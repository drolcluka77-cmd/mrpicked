FAQ

Questions owners ask us

What is GEO, in plain words?

Making sure AI assistants like ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity know your company, describe it correctly and recommend it. It builds on local SEO, so you also rank better on Google.

Can AI really send me tows?

Drivers increasingly ask ChatGPT, Gemini or Google’s AI for a towing service instead of scrolling results. AI names the companies whose details it trusts: correct 24/7 hours, number, service area and good reviews. That is exactly what we fix and keep up to date.

How soon will I see results?

Fixes to your Google profile and listings often show within weeks. AI answers change more slowly; we measure every month and promise a higher score in 90 days.

Does the WhatsApp concierge replace my dispatcher?

No. It answers first, collects location, vehicle and photos, and sends an estimate from your price list. Your dispatcher confirms and sends the truck.

Can I cancel?

Yes, monthly with 60 days’ notice. No long contracts.
